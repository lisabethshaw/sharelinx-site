Privacy Policy

ShareLinx is designed to help you save and organize links. We've written this policy in plain language to explain what information ShareLinx works with, where it's stored, and the choices you have. In short: ShareLinx is built to keep your saved links on your own device and under your control.

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Information We Collect

ShareLinx does not require you to create a ShareLinx account, and we do not operate our own user account system. Core use of the app does not require signing up or signing in to ShareLinx.

The app stores the content you choose to save, which may include:

  • The web addresses (URLs) you save
  • Titles associated with those links
  • Notes you add
  • Collections you create to organize your links
  • Link metadata, such as descriptions or images
  • Optional summaries, when that feature is supported and used

This information is stored on your device. ShareLinx does not collect this content into its own servers.

How We Use Information

The information you save is used only to provide the app's features: saving your links, organizing them into Collections, displaying titles and details, storing your notes, and helping you revisit your saved pages. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your saved links for advertising.

iCloud Sync

If iCloud is enabled for ShareLinx, your ShareLinx data may sync across your devices through Apple iCloud using your Apple account. This syncing is handled by Apple's iCloud service and is subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices. You can manage iCloud availability for ShareLinx through your device's Apple/iCloud settings.

Shared Collections

ShareLinx lets you share a Collection with people you choose. When you share a Collection, the links, sections, comments, and reactions in that Collection become visible to the people you invite, and what they add becomes visible to you. Sharing is handled through Apple iCloud using your Apple account and Apple's standard iCloud sharing system, and is subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Sharing is always something you start. Your other links, Collections, and personal notes are never included in a shared Collection — only the Collection you choose to share. Whoever owns a shared Collection can change who has access or stop sharing, and anyone invited can leave at any time. If you'd rather not share, ShareLinx works fully as a private, personal app.

Within a shared Collection, the people you've invited can see your name (as provided by Apple's iCloud sharing) next to the links, comments, and reactions you add, along with when you added them. Your personal notes on a link are not shared with Shared Collection participants, even for links in a shared Collection. Separately, you can choose to include notes in optional AI Access, as described below.

Contacts

To show you the names and photos of people you share with, ShareLinx can match the people in a shared Collection against your device's Contacts. This matching happens entirely on your device. No contact information is uploaded, synced, or shared with other participants — each person sees their own Contacts' names and photos, never yours. ShareLinx only reads Contacts if you grant permission, and works fully without it.

Notifications and Preferences

Notifications for shared Collections are off until you turn them on. Your choices — which Collections, which kinds of activity, and which people you do or don't want to hear from — are stored in your own private iCloud database so they follow you across your devices, and they are never visible to other participants.

AI Summaries

When supported and available on your device, ShareLinx can generate optional summaries, tags, and topics for saved pages. These are created on your device using Apple's Foundation Models and Apple Intelligence. This feature is optional, and you can use ShareLinx fully without it.

Optional AI Access

AI Access is separate from ShareLinx's on-device summaries. It is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings → AI Access, you choose which Collections or tags may be accessed. Eligible links matching either selection can be included, including links you later add to those Collections or tags. Archived links are excluded. These permissions apply on the device where you configure them; you must configure access separately on another device.

Approved data can include link titles and URLs, saved summaries, tags and topics, selected Collection names and identifiers, and dates and other saved organizational metadata. Personal notes are excluded unless you turn on “Include personal notes.” Link preview image and icon data are not included in the AI link export. Review your selection before enabling access.

On Mac, ShareLinx writes an approved snapshot to an AIAccess.json file in a folder you choose. The included local bridge lets an AI app you configure read that snapshot. ShareLinx refreshes the export as relevant library data or permissions change while the app is running. Treat the export as a copy of the approved content: other software with access to the folder can read it, and choosing a synced or shared folder may make it available through that folder's service or sharing settings.

On supported devices, the AI-oriented JSON actions in Shortcuts also apply the device's AI Access selections. You can choose to pass their output to another app. These controls govern ShareLinx's AI-access interfaces; they do not revoke copies you separately share, copy, or export.

ShareLinx does not send this export to a ShareLinx-operated AI server. However, an AI app or automation you connect may send the content it reads to its own cloud service, retain it in conversation history, or use it according to that provider's settings and privacy policy. This can include URLs and personal notes if you approved them. Review the receiving service's privacy and retention settings before connecting it. On-device processing of ShareLinx summaries does not mean an external AI connection stays on your device.

To stop access, turn off “Allow AI access” or choose “Disconnect AI Access” in Settings. ShareLinx stops returning approved data through its AI-access actions and attempts to remove its current Mac export. If the export cannot be removed, Settings displays an error: disconnect the AI client and delete the exported copy before relying on revocation. You can also remove the ShareLinx connection from your AI client's settings. Disconnecting or deleting library content cannot recall information already read, copied, backed up, or retained by another app or service; manage those copies with the recipient or provider.

Share Sheet Suggestions

To help ShareLinx appear among the suggestions in your share sheet, the app may record on-device Siri suggestions when you save a link. These suggestions stay on your device and are managed by iOS; ShareLinx never receives them.

Link Metadata

To display useful details about your saved links, ShareLinx may fetch information from the webpages you save. This can include titles, images, descriptions, or readable text, which may also be used to generate on-device summaries. This metadata is used to provide app functionality and to help you recognize and revisit your saved links.

Data Sharing

ShareLinx does not sell your personal data, and we do not use your saved links for advertising. Widgets and share extensions associated with ShareLinx may access your saved app data only to provide app functionality, such as letting you save a new link or view existing ones. If you use iCloud sync, your data is handled by Apple as described above. If you enable optional AI Access, approved content can also be read by the AI apps or automations you connect, as described in the Optional AI Access section.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your saved links and Collections remain stored until you remove them. You can delete saved links and Collections directly in the app at any time. If iCloud sync is enabled, deletions are reflected across your synced devices through Apple iCloud. "Delete All ShareLinx Data" in Settings removes your links and Collections from your device and from iCloud, deletes the shared Collections you own, and leaves the ones you've joined.

Children's Privacy

ShareLinx is a general-purpose link-saving tool and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not require an account and stores data on your device, ShareLinx does not gather personal details beyond the link content you choose to save.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
