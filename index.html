What ShareLinx does
01

Save from anywhere

When you find something worth keeping, use the share sheet in Safari or any app to send it straight to ShareLinx. No copying, no pasting, no switching back and forth.

02

Organize with Collections

Group your links into Collections that make sense to you — "Weeknight Dinners," "Project Research," "Things to Buy Later." A single link can live in more than one Collection, so you never have to choose just one place for it.

03

Add your own notes

Capture why a link mattered. Jot down a quick thought, a reminder, or the detail you'll want when you return to it, so your saved pages stay meaningful instead of mysterious.

04

Browse the way you like

Open ShareLinx on iPhone for quick saves on the go, spread out on iPad or Mac for easy organizing, or enjoy a spacious view on Apple Vision Pro. Your links stay in step across all of them.

05

Optional on-device AI summaries

On supported Apple Intelligence devices, ShareLinx can generate summaries right on your device, giving you the gist of a saved page at a glance. It's entirely optional and processed locally.

06

Sync with iCloud

Use iCloud sync, if enabled, to keep your links in step across your Apple devices. Your saved links remain yours, and syncing is handled through your Apple account.

New · Plan together with Shared Collections
07

Share a collection

Invite family or friends to a collection so everyone can add links and keep one shared list — a trip to plan, a project to research, or gift ideas to pool together.

08

Comment and react

Talk it over right on a link with threaded comments, and react with a tap — emoji, Genmoji, and stickers. Mention someone when you want their take.

09

Organize together

Group shared links into sections, or start from a template like Trip planning — food and drink, lodging, activities, travel, and more.

10

Schedule and stay in the loop

Build a shared schedule and add it to your calendar. Notifications and an Activity inbox keep everyone caught up on new links, comments, and reactions.

Get started
