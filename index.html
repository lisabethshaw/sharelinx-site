What ShareLinx does
01

Save from anywhere

When you find something worth keeping, use the share sheet in Safari or any app to send it straight to ShareLinx. No copying, no pasting, no switching back and forth.

02

Organize with Collections

Group your links into Collections that make sense to you — "Weeknight Dinners," "Project Research," "Things to Buy Later" — each with its own emoji or symbol. A single link can live in more than one Collection, so you never have to choose just one place for it.

03

Add your own notes

Capture why a link mattered. Jot down a quick thought, a reminder, or the detail you'll want when you return to it, so your saved pages stay meaningful instead of mysterious.

04

Browse the way you like

Open ShareLinx on iPhone for quick saves on the go, spread out on iPad or Mac for easy organizing, or enjoy a spacious view on Apple Vision Pro. Your links stay in step across all of them.

05

Optional on-device AI summaries

On supported Apple Intelligence devices, ShareLinx can generate summaries, tags, and topics right on your device, giving you the gist of a saved page at a glance. Summary generation is optional and runs on your device. Connecting an external AI app is a separate, opt-in feature.

06

Sync with iCloud

Use iCloud sync, if enabled, to keep your links in step across your Apple devices. Your saved links remain yours, and syncing is handled through your Apple account.

New in 2.0 · Plan together with Shared Collections
07

Share a collection

Invite family or friends to a collection so everyone can add links and keep one shared list — a trip to plan, a project to research, or gift ideas to pool together. Every link shows who added it and when, with the faces you know from your own Contacts.

08

Comment and react

Talk it over right on a link with threaded comments that are easy to follow — names, photos, and times on every reply. React with a tap — emoji, Genmoji, and stickers — and see exactly who reacted and when.

09

Organize together

Group shared links into sections, or start from a template like Trip planning — food and drink, lodging, activities, travel, and more.

10

Quiet, unless you ask

ShareLinx never notifies you by default. Turn on exactly what you want — per collection, per kind of event, even per person — and a tap on any notification lands you right on the comment it's about.

Connect your AI app, if you choose

Optional AI access lets a connected app read links from collections or tags you approve. Personal notes are excluded unless you include them. On Mac, use the built-in connection setup. The receiving AI app may send approved content to its cloud service and retain copies. Learn how to control AI access and read our Privacy Policy.
